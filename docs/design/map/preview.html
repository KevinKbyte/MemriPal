Kendall/MIT to Meta office walk

About 600 m, about 8 min walk

  1. Kendall/MIT station: exit onto Main St and walk east toward the One Broadway tower
  2. One Broadway tower (Brothers Marketplace at its base): bear left onto Third St
  3. Watermark Kendall towers at Kendall St / Potter St: keep straight on Third St
  4. Third Square Apartments at Munroe St / Linskey Way: keep straight on Third St
  5. Kendall Building (old brick boiler works) at Binney St: turn right onto Binney St
  6. Meta office, 100 Binney St: on your right, Toomey Park across the street
