    What makes a good palace room

    • A room you could walk through with your eyes shut: your living room, office or kitchen.
    • Furniture and objects with clear, different shapes. Ten chairs make a poor palace.
    • Daylight or every lamp on, and nobody moving through the shot.

    3. Choosing 10 to 12 distinct objects
